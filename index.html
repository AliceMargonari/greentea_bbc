The Health Benefits of Green Tea, Explained


Matcha is a type of green tea made from specially grown leaves of the Camellia sinensis plant. Matcha is prepared by whisking finely ground matcha powder into hot water or milk.

Green tea is well-studied for its health benefits, and matcha is basically a more concentrated version. Matcha is rich in antioxidants — substances that help protect your body from cell damage and lower the risk of chronic diseases. Green tea and matcha are closely related, and green tea can help with weight loss because of its high levels of antioxidants. Studies show that green tea can boost your metabolism and increase fat burning, thanks to its caffeine content.

Other studies have linked certain compounds in matcha — including antioxidants and other components like chlorophyll, caffeine, and L-theanine — suggests that it may offer also cognitive benefits.
